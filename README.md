# TOMA: Quiet Luxury

Implement the requested luxury clothing website for the user's brand "TOMA" now; use internal planning and do not present another implementation plan for user approval.

### Brand & Vision
- **Brand Name:** TOMA
- **Concept:** Quiet luxury menswear inspired by the understated elegance, earth-toned palette, and craftsmanship-first philosophy of Brunello Cucinelli.
- **Tone & Palette:** Warm neutrals — cashmere taupe, ivory, oatmeal, warm sand, charcoal, espresso, and soft slate. Editorial serif typography paired with clean, tracked sans-serif details.

### Product Categories (Men's Collection)
1. **Shirts:** Linen spread-collar shirts, cashmere knit polos, fine cotton poplin overshirts, silk-cotton henleys.
2. **Pants:** Relaxed pleated linen-wool trousers, brushed cotton drawstring chinos, virgin wool flannel tailored pants.
3. **Shoes:** Suede penny loafers, minimalist leather court sneakers, Goodyear-welted Chelsea boots in soft calfskin.
4. **Bags:** Full-grain leather weekend duffle, minimalist suede tote, pebbled leather crossbody messenger, document folio.

### Product Specifications (Must-Have on Every Product)
Every product detail view must include a rich, structured specifications breakdown:
- **Fabric & Composition:** Exact material blends (e.g. 100% Mongolian Cashmere, 70% Linen / 30% Mulberry Silk, Full-Grain Calfskin Suede).
- **Fit & Silhouette:** Fit notes (e.g. Relaxed Italian drape, Tailored taper) and model measurements.
- **Finishing & Hardware:** Details like mother-of-pearl or horn buttons, hand-stitched pick stitching, Raccagni zips, Vibram rubber soles.
- **Care Instructions:** Professional dry clean, cold hand wash, leather conditioning guide.
- **Provenance:** Origin and workshop notes (e.g. Crafted in Italy, artisanal family-owned workshop).
- **Available Sizes:** Standard sizing with interactive size selector and stock hints.

### Core Experience & Pages
- **Minimalist Header:** Clean TOMA wordmark, category tabs (All, Shirts, Pants, Shoes, Bags), search dialog, wishlist counter, and slide-out shopping bag.
- **Hero Editorial Showcase:** Full-bleed lookbook banner with seasonal collection statement and call to explore.
- **Product Grid & Filtering:** Filter by category, noble material (Cashmere, Linen, Wool, Leather/Suede), color palette, and size. Cards feature smooth hover image swap, color swatches, and quick add.
- **Product Detail Experience:** Multi-image gallery, interactive specs accordion/tabs, size picker, and "Complete the Look" companion styling recommendations.
- **Slide-Out Bag Drawer:** Cart item management, free insured shipping and complimentary luxury packaging indicators, simulated checkout.
- **Brand Storytelling Section:** "The TOMA Philosophy" highlighting natural fibers, humanistic craftsmanship, and timeless design.
